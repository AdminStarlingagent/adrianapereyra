Meet Adriana

A REALTOR® who spent twenty years on the lending side

Before real estate, Adriana spent two decades in the mortgage industry, so she reads a transaction the way a lender does. She knows which offers will actually fund, where financing tends to fall apart, and how to keep a closing on schedule.

She is a certified Military Relocation Professional and luxury home specialist, with added training in negotiation (RENE), buyer representation (ABR®), resort and second-home properties (RSPS), and short sales and foreclosures (SFR®).

You work with Adriana directly, in English, Spanish, or Italian, from the first conversation to the day you close.
